[03]About

I've spent nine years untangling
the software people use at work, for 4.2M people a month: payroll, physio bookings, furniture and mountain huts.

I start where the problem happens: on the reception desk, in the pay run, at the trailhead. Then I design the fewest decisions that get someone from stuck to done, and I stay until it ships.

Before going independent I designed at Fietsbank and Harbour Labs. Today I lead design at Orrin and take one outside project at a time.
